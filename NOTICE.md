# Notice

## Milkdrop presets

SpectraVisio plays Milkdrop presets through [Butterchurn](https://github.com/jberg/butterchurn) (MIT, Jordan Berg). The app includes the 100 presets of the main pack of [`butterchurn-presets`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/butterchurn-presets) 2.4.7, which are bundled into the published build and load only when Milkdrop is used.

- The MIT license of Butterchurn and of `butterchurn-presets` covers their code, not the presets.
- The presets were made by members of the Milkdrop community (Geiss, Rovastar, Aderrasi, Flexi, Martin, Zylot and others). The author is in each preset name, which the app shows in its list.
- No license for the presets was found in the package or in its sources. They are not covered by this project's GPL-3.0 license and remain the work of their authors.

To have a preset removed, open an issue at https://github.com/memoriainfinita/Spectra-visio/issues naming it. It will be taken out of the build.
